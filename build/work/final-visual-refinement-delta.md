## Final visual review outcome

The final-pass rendering removes the remaining dominant visual signals of a generic auto-layout: the shared participant network is tighter, labels use smaller local gaps from sampled points on their own structural link, directional marks are shorter and lighter, and self loops are compact local arcs. The six outputs retain the lecturer-reference essentials—plain background, unfilled underlined object rectangles, free numbered labels, thin calm connectors, and small direction marks—without modern cards, lifelines, activation bars, fragments, or decorative framing.

The only unavoidable difference from the four-object booklet example is source-required scenario density. CD-02, CD-05, and CD-06 contain substantially more approved messages and participants. Their message runs are therefore multiline, but are kept local to their corresponding link corridors and pass semantic and geometry gates.
